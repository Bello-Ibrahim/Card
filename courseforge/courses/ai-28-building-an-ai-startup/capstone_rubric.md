# Capstone Rubric: Validated AI Startup Concept, MVP Demo and Pitch Deck

## Task
Deliver a validated AI startup concept with an MVP demo and a 10-slide pitch deck. You build it in three steps: a 2-minute MVP demo and a one-page validation summary (L13, capstone step 1), a 10-slide pitch deck in Canva (L14, capstone step 2), and pitch practice with Claude as a sceptical investor, followed by final improvements and submission (L15, capstone step 3). Use your work from earlier lessons: interviews (L03), demand test (L04), MVP scope and prototype (L06, L07), test set (L08), unit economics and business model (L09, L10) and risk checklist (L11). Use fake data in your demo and remove names and personal details from all notes. Prices may be placeholders, as long as they are clearly labelled. Nothing in this course is legal advice.

## Deliverables
- A one-page validation summary: your problem statement, the number of interviews and the top three patterns with evidence, your demand test with its success threshold and result, your test-set pass rate, and one thing you learned that changed your product.
- A recorded MVP demo of about 2 minutes, showing one user completing the core job with fake data.
- Your test-set sheet with at least 15 cases and two scored rounds, and a one-sentence fallback plan.
- Your unit economics sheet (average and heavy customer, gross margin, CAC and payback) and a comparison of two business models.
- Your risk checklist with levels and next steps.
- A 10-slide pitch deck (PDF or view-only link) with the slides: problem, solution, demo, customer evidence, market, business model, moat, competition, team and the ask.
- A short record of your sceptical-investor practice: at least five questions, your three weakest answers, and the slides you improved.

## Submission Checklist
- My validation summary fits on one page and uses real numbers from my interviews and demand test.
- I set my demand-test threshold before running the test.
- My notes and demo contain no names, personal details or confidential data.
- My demo is about 2 minutes and shows one user completing the core job.
- My test set has at least 15 cases, including cases that should be refused or sent to a human.
- My unit economics sheet uses formulas and includes an average and a heavy customer.
- All prices are either checked on the day or clearly labelled as placeholders.
- My risk checklist gives a next step for every high risk, and I understand it is not legal advice.
- My deck has exactly 10 slides, with a moat slide that goes beyond the AI model and a specific ask.
- I practised with Claude as a sceptical investor and improved at least two slides.
