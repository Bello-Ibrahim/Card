# Capstone Rubric: AI Use Case Proposal with Risk and Compliance Assessment

## Task
Design an AI use case proposal for a financial institution, including a risk and compliance assessment. You scope the proposal in L13 (capstone step 1) and complete the risk and compliance assessment and recommendation in L14 (capstone step 2). Use a real type of institution but hypothetical details, and only synthetic or described data. Never paste real customer or confidential data into an AI tool. The proposal must not give investment advice.

## Deliverables
- A proposal of 2–4 pages using the seven-section template: problem, business value, users, data, model approach, human oversight and success measures.
- A risk register in Google Sheets with at least 6 risks covering fairness, privacy, explainability, security, third-party and operational risk, each with likelihood, impact, score, control, owner and residual rating.
- A principles check: one line for each of the six regulatory principles from L11, with what must be confirmed locally.
- A recommendation paragraph: go, pilot or no-go, with reasons; a pilot also needs scope, length, measures, a stop rule and a named decision-maker role.
- A short AI-use log: what you asked an AI assistant, what it suggested, and what you checked or changed.

## Submission Checklist
- My proposal covers all seven template sections and has a narrow, specific scope.
- I used only synthetic or described data and pasted no real customer or confidential data into any AI tool.
- My risk register has at least 6 risks and covers fairness, privacy, explainability, security, third-party and operational risk.
- Every risk has a score, a specific control, an owner role and a residual rating.
- I completed a principles check for all six principles and marked named laws as examples to confirm locally.
- My success measures include at least one fairness or customer-outcome measure.
- My recommendation is go, pilot or no-go, with reasons; a pilot has an end date, a stop rule and a named decision-maker role.
- I included an AI-use log and checked every fact and number the AI suggested.
- My proposal contains no investment advice.
