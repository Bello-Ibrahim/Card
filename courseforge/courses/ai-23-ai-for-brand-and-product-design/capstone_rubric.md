# Capstone Rubric: AI-Assisted Brand Identity and App Flow Case Study

## Task
Design a brand identity and a key app screen flow with an AI-assisted process, and document it as a case study that shows where AI helped, where you overruled it and which risks you checked. You build the brand guidelines in L10 (capstone step 1), the screens in L11 (step 2), the prototype in L12 (step 3), the critique and asset log in L13 (step 4) and the case study in L14 (step 5). Use a real side project or a fictional business. Do not include confidential client data, participant names or faces without consent, or prompts that name living artists or existing brands.

## Deliverables
- A one-page brand guidelines frame in Figma: logo (redrawn as a vector, with clear space, minimum size and incorrect uses), a palette of 5 colours with tested text pairs, a type pairing, and 2 example applications, all built with styles, variables or components.
- A key app flow of 4 to 6 screens rebuilt with your components, plus a drafts page showing the AI drafts.
- A clickable Figma prototype link, including at least one error state and one empty state, and a short note from a one-person task-based test.
- A completed critique checklist with the top 3 fixes, and an asset log with source, tool, changes, checks and status for every asset.
- A case study page in Figma with five sections: problem, process, AI use log, decisions and outcomes.

## Submission Checklist
- My brand guidelines page includes logo usage, 5 colours with tested text pairs, type and 2 applications, built with styles, variables or components.
- My logo is redrawn by hand as a vector, not auto-traced, and it is marked "trademark and ownership checks recommended".
- My name shortlist has language and domain checks, and no name is described as legally cleared.
- None of my prompts name living artists or existing brands.
- My 4 to 6 screens use my own components, and my drafts page shows what AI produced.
- My prototype link works and includes at least one error state and one empty state.
- I tested the flow with one person who agreed to take part, and I fixed at least one problem.
- I verified every point from Claude's review and every contrast pair myself.
- My asset log lists every asset with its source, tool, changes, checks and status.
- My case study has all five sections and shows at least one place where I overruled AI.
- I did not include confidential client data, participant names or faces without consent.
