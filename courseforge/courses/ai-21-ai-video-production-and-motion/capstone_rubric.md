# Capstone Rubric: AI-Assisted Brand or Explainer Video

## Task
Produce a 60 to 90 second AI-assisted brand or explainer video, from script to final export, for a real or fictional product or organisation. You build it in four steps: a rough cut with captions (L11), sound, music and two exports (L12), a pre-publish checklist (L13), and the final export with a half-page production note (L14). Use stock avatars, stock voices and fictional characters only. Do not create an avatar or voice of any real person, and do not paste personal or confidential data into AI tools.

## Deliverables
- The final video, 60 to 90 seconds long, in two versions: 16:9 and 9:16.
- Your two-column script and your shot list, with duration, shot type, camera movement, source and a status (keep, trim, regenerate or replace) for every shot.
- A half-page production note: tools used and their roles, 2 or 3 key prompts shown exactly, the main problems and how you solved them, and your pre-publish checks.
- Your completed pre-publish checklist, with the music licence saved and notes on each tool's commercial-use terms.

## Submission Checklist
- My final video is 60 to 90 seconds long, and I exported a 16:9 and a 9:16 version.
- I submitted my script and my shot list with a status for every shot.
- My key prompts are shown exactly in my production note.
- I used only stock avatars, stock voices and fictional characters, or real people who gave clear consent.
- I read and corrected every caption line, and captions sit inside the safe area.
- Every word of the voice is clear over the music on phone speakers.
- I saved my music licence and checked each tool's commercial-use terms for my plan.
- My video discloses AI use clearly, and no scene could mislead a viewer.
- My production note lists the tools, key prompts, problems solved and pre-publish checks.
