# Capstone Rubric: AI-Assisted Podcast Episode or Narrated Video

## Task
Produce a 2 to 4 minute podcast episode or narrated video with AI-assisted voice, clean-up and music. You mix a first draft in L09 (capstone step 1) and review, caption and submit the final version in L10 (capstone step 2). "AI-assisted" means at least two of: a text-to-speech voiceover, Whisper captions, AI or plugin-based clean-up, and an AI music bed. Do not use a cloned voice of anyone else, and do not name living artists in music prompts.

## Deliverables
- The final episode (MP3 or WAV) or narrated video, 2 to 4 minutes long.
- Captions from the final audio: an SRT file, or burned-in captions plus the SRT.
- A rights log with one row for every voice and music asset: tool and plan, short licence summary, attribution, consent status and date checked.
- A disclosure line that states specifically where AI was used, as it appears in the show notes, description or credits.
- The completed production checklist from L10.

## Submission Checklist
- My episode or video is 2 to 4 minutes long and uses at least two kinds of AI assistance.
- I did not clone anyone else's voice, and any real person's voice has written consent for this use.
- My music prompts describe mood, genre, tempo and instruments, and name no artists or songs.
- The voice is clear over the music on headphones and on a phone speaker.
- I set the final loudness by following the platform's current guidance.
- My captions were made from the final audio and checked by hand for names and numbers.
- My rights log has a dated row for every voice and music asset, with licence terms and credits.
- My disclosure line says specifically where AI was used.
- I did not paste personal or confidential information into any online AI tool.
