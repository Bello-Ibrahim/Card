**Steps**

2. Open the free plan limits and the licence page, then return to Create
3. Type the prompt: 'Calm and curious, light Latin folk, about 90 BPM, nylon-string guitar and soft marimba, light shaker, instrumental, no vocals, 30 seconds, gentle ending.'

## Clip 2: scene 10

- **Filename:** `ai-22-ai-voice-audio-and-music_L07_screen_2.mp4`
- **Target length:** about 19 seconds

**Steps**

1. Click Create and wait for the options
2. Generate until there are three options, A, B and C, then download each one
3. In Audacity, import the voiceover and the three options onto separate tracks
4. Lower the gain of each music track so the voice is clearly on top

**Narration over this clip (for pacing)**

> She generates three options and downloads each one. Each option sounds a little different, even from the same prompt. Then she imports her voiceover and each option into Audacity, on separate tracks. She lowers the music track, so she can hear her voice clearly.
