# Capstone Rubric: AI-Assisted Unit of Study

## Task
Build a complete AI-assisted unit of study of 3 to 5 lessons for learners you teach or train. The unit includes lesson plans, differentiated and accessible materials, a quiz, an assessment rubric and a unit AI policy, reviewed for accuracy, bias and accessibility. You build the quiz in L09 (step 1), the rubric in L10 (step 2), the unit plan in L11 (step 3), and you complete and review the pack in L12 (step 4). Never include learner names, grades or other personal data in any part of the pack or in any AI tool.

## Deliverables
- A unit plan using the course template: 2 to 4 observable unit outcomes, a lesson sequence of 3 to 5 lessons, and a completed alignment table.
- Full timed lesson plans for at least 2 lessons, with your own edits marked.
- Differentiated materials: at least one text or task in support, core and challenge versions that keep the same key points.
- At least one accessible visual material (worksheet, slides or infographic) with alt text for every meaningful image.
- A self-marking Google Forms quiz of about 10 questions, with a short table of the items you fixed.
- An analytic rubric for the unit's main assessment task, with a note on how you tested it on a sample.
- A one-page traffic-light AI policy for the unit's tasks.
- A review record (accuracy, bias and representation, accessibility, alignment) and a short AI-use note in a table: part, how AI helped, what you changed.

## Submission Checklist
- My unit plan has 2 to 4 observable outcomes, 3 to 5 lessons and a completed alignment table.
- I included full timed plans for at least 2 lessons, with my edits marked.
- My support, core and challenge versions all keep the same key points.
- My visual material passes the 5-point accessibility checklist, and every meaningful image has alt text.
- My Google Forms quiz works, and I recorded which items I fixed.
- My rubric criteria link to outcomes, and I tested the rubric on a sample, not on real learner work with personal data.
- My unit AI policy uses the traffic-light model and matches the tasks in my lesson plans.
- I reviewed the whole pack for accuracy, bias and representation, accessibility and alignment, and recorded my fixes.
- There are no learner names, grades or personal data anywhere in the pack.
- My AI-use note says, for each part, how AI helped and what I changed.
