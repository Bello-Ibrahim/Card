## Production notes for this lesson

- [VERSION] Record the Google Forms screen scenes on the live tool shortly before release and check: 'Make this a quiz', release of marks, feedback options, the Answer key button, points, Multiple choice, Shuffle option order and Preview. The voiceover names settings in general words so it survives small interface changes.
- [REGION] Rules on collecting learners' names and email addresses in online forms differ by country and institution. The voiceover tells learners to collect only what they need and check their institution's rules; do not show a form that collects real learner details.
- Screen scenes 10 to 13 use Google Forms with a demo Google account; hide the account name, profile photo and any other forms or files.
- Chen Wei and his college in Kuala Lumpur are fictional; stock footage must show no real college name or logo.
- The depreciation example is for illustration: a van bought for 20,000 over 5 years with the straight-line method gives 4,000 per year (assuming no residual value). No currency is named on purpose.
