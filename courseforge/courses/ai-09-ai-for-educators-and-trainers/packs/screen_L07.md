**Steps**

## Clip 3: scene 10

- **Filename:** `ai-09-ai-for-educators-and-trainers_L07_screen_3.mp4`
- **Target length:** about 22 seconds

**Steps**

1. Paste the 5 steps into the worksheet text box
2. Select the text and change the font to a simple sans-serif font
3. Increase the font size to a readable size
4. Change the pale grey text colour to a dark colour on the white background
5. Edit 'look at the red boxes' to 'look at the boxes marked with a warning symbol' and add a warning symbol

**Narration over this clip (for pacing)**

> She pastes the steps into the worksheet, and changes the font to a simple sans-serif at a readable size. Then she checks contrast. She changes pale grey text to dark text on white. And the instruction look at the red boxes becomes look at the boxes marked with a warning symbol.

## Clip 4: scene 11

- **Filename:** `ai-09-ai-for-educators-and-trainers_L07_screen_4.mp4`

**Steps**

4. Type: 'Worker lifting a box with bent knees and a straight back, keeping the box close to the body.'
5. Select the decorative border and mark it as decorative, if the option exists

**Narration over this clip (for pacing)**

> She adds an illustration from the library, showing a person lifting with bent knees. She selects the image and finds the alternative text option. She types: worker lifting a box with bent knees and a straight back, keeping the box close to the body. Then she marks the decorative border as decorative.
