# Capstone Rubric: Audit an AI Tool for Bias and Safety Risks

## Task
Test a real AI tool for bias and safety risks with structured prompts, rate the risks you find, and write a short audit report with clear recommendations. You run the paired-prompt tests in L08 (capstone step 1), rate the risks in L09 (step 2) and write the report in L10 (step 3). Claude or ChatGPT (free versions) is fine.

Three rules apply to every audit:
- Follow the tool's terms of use.
- Never enter real personal or confidential data in test prompts. Use invented names, CVs and cases only.
- Describe your findings as limited tests, not proof of bias. This is a practical audit, not a legal review.

## Deliverables
- A 1–2 page audit report using the template from L10: scope, test design, paired prompts, findings, severity, limitations and recommendations.
- An appendix with your full paired-prompt table: at least 5 pairs, each changing one detail, at least 3 different details tested, at least 3 runs per prompt, with the exact prompts and answers.
- Your likelihood-and-impact risk matrix with at least 5 risks across at least 3 types (bias, misinformation, privacy, safety) and your top 3 marked.
- At least 3 recommendations that are specific, realistic and assigned to a person or team.

## Submission Checklist
- I followed the tool's terms of use.
- I used only invented names and details. No real personal or confidential data appears in any prompt.
- I ran at least 5 paired prompts, each changing only one detail, with at least 3 runs per prompt in new chats.
- I tested at least 3 different details, such as name, age, country, gender or disability.
- My full paired-prompt table is in the appendix, with exact prompts and answers.
- I rated at least 5 risks for likelihood and impact, with a reason for each, and marked my top 3.
- My report uses all 7 template sections and fits on 1–2 pages.
- I described my findings as limited tests, not proof of bias, and listed my limitations.
- I wrote at least 3 recommendations that are specific, realistic and assigned to a person or team.
