# Capstone Rubric: Support Assistant Prototype with Human Hand-off

## Task
Build a working support assistant prototype for a made-up company, with a hand-off-to-human flow, and plan how you will measure it. You build the assistant in L09 (capstone step 1), add the hand-off flow in L10 (step 2), test and improve it in L11 (step 3), and write a one-page launch note in L12 (step 4). Use a free chatbot builder of your choice and, if needed, the free plan of Zapier or a cloud trial of n8n. Use only made-up company content and test data. Do not put real customer data or confidential documents into free tools.

## Deliverables
- A working assistant in test mode that covers at least 3 topics from your knowledge base, with a share link or screenshots of its setup and a test chat.
- A completed hand-off flow template (trigger, collect, send, tell) with at least 4 triggers, including "customer asks for a person" and at least one of "refund above a limit" or "legal or safety issue".
- Evidence that the hand-off works: a screenshot of a hand-off summary that arrived in a test inbox, ticket or sheet.
- A test sheet with about 20 test questions, scores for accuracy, tone and hand-off before and after your fixes, and a completed 8-point test-conversation checklist.
- A one-page launch note with scope, hand-off rules, measures, warning signs, quality checks, transparency and privacy, and a review schedule.

## Criteria
| Criterion | Objective | Excellent | Good | Developing | Not yet | Points |

| Hand-off rules and workflow design | O4 | At least 4 clear triggers (including a request for a person and a money, legal or safety limit); each linked to a reason about risk, emotion, complexity or vulnerability; flow template fully completed. | 4 triggers with most reasons; flow template mostly complete. | Fewer than 4 triggers, or triggers are vague or hard to apply. | No hand-off rules. | 20 |
| Working hand-off flow | O4, O6 | Summary reaches a real human channel in all test cases; contains need, trigger, details, what was said, mood and urgency; customer is told who will reply, how and when, including out of hours. | Summary arrives in most tests and is mostly complete; customer message is clear. | Hand-off starts but the summary is missing, incomplete or goes nowhere a person would see it. | No hand-off flow. | 20 |

## Submission Checklist
- My assistant covers at least 3 topics and I have a share link or screenshots of the setup and a test chat.
- The welcome message says it is an AI assistant and that the customer can ask for a person.
- I completed the hand-off flow template: trigger, collect, send and tell.
- My hand-off rules include "customer asks for a person" and at least one money, legal or safety trigger.
- I have a screenshot of a hand-off summary that arrived in a test inbox, ticket or sheet.
- My test sheet has about 20 questions of different types, with scores before and after my fixes.
- I used the 8-point test-conversation checklist.
- My launch note lists the measures, warning signs, quality checks, transparency and privacy points, and a review schedule, with no invented target numbers.
- I used only made-up company content and test data, and no real customer data.
