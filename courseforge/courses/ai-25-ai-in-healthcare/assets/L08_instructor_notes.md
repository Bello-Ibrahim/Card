# L08 Instructor Notes (do not give to learners)

For use with `L08_synthetic_discharge_note.md`. Keep this file separate so it is never pasted into an AI tool with the note.

Key facts a learner's list should include (8–10): diagnosis (pneumonia, **right** lower lobe); **penicillin allergy (rash)**; non-penicillin antibiotic, changed to tablets on Day 3, course to be completed; oxygen stopped Day 3; length of stay 5 days; raised blood glucose, **possible** diabetes (not confirmed); family doctor to check blood glucose within two weeks; **repeat chest X-ray in six weeks**; blood culture result **pending**; usual medicines unchanged, no new regular medicines.

Errors to watch for in AI summaries, matching the L08 failure types:

- **Omission:** the blood glucose check, the pending blood culture, or the allergy.
- **Invention:** any named antibiotic (especially a penicillin-type one such as amoxicillin, which would be unsafe for this patient), any dose, or a new regular medicine.
- **Distortion:** "left" instead of right lower lobe; "six months" instead of six weeks; "chest pain" instead of no chest pain.
- **Loss of uncertainty:** "possible diabetes" becoming "diabetes" or "newly diagnosed diabetes".
