# Approved Source Text: Preparing for Your Clinic Visit

**Fictional service:** Brookfield Community Clinic (a hypothetical clinic)
**Approved by (fictional):** Dr. Amara Okafor, Clinic Lead
**Version:** 1.0 (teaching sample)

Use only the facts below when drafting reminders or leaflets in the L07 exercise. Any fact that is not in this text is **not approved**.

## Before your visit

1. Your appointment letter or text message shows the date, the time and the room.
2. Please arrive 15 minutes before your appointment time so that reception can check your details.
3. If you cannot come, please call the clinic at least one day before, so another patient can use the appointment.

## What to bring

1. Your appointment letter or text message.
2. An identity document or your clinic card.
3. A list of all the medicines you take, including ones you buy yourself. You can also bring the medicine boxes.
4. Your glasses or hearing aid, if you use them.
5. A short list of the questions you want to ask.

## Your medicines

Keep taking your medicines as usual unless a member of the clinic team has told you something different. If you have a question about your medicines, call the clinic and ask to speak to a nurse. Do not stop or change a medicine on your own.

## Language and support

You can ask for an interpreter when you book. You can bring a family member or friend with you.

## Contact

- **Clinic phone (fictional):** 000 000 0000, Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:00.
- **For questions about your health or your medicines,** ask to speak to a nurse.
- **In an emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for your appointment.**
