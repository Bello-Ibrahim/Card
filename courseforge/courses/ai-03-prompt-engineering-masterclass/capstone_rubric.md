# Capstone Rubric: Personal Prompt Library

## Task
Build a documented library of 15 tested, reusable prompts for your own job role. You choose your tasks and write your first three templates in L13 (capstone step 1), write and test all 15 in L14 (capstone step 2), and document them for a colleague in L15 (capstone step 3). Use only invented or non-confidential material in every prompt, example and test. Do not paste personal data, client details or internal secrets into any AI tool, and follow your organisation's AI policy.

## Deliverables
- A list of 15 recurring tasks from your job role, each with its prompt pattern (draft, rewrite, summarise or extract, classify, analyse, or interview first).
- 15 prompt templates with clear placeholders, each on a prompt card with these fields: name, version, purpose, pattern, prompt template, inputs, example input, example output, test notes, and the tool and date of the last test.
- Test notes showing that each template was tested at least twice with different inputs (one typical case and one harder case), checked with the four-part checklist (facts, sources, bias, confidentiality), and scored from 1 to 5 on accuracy, format and tone.
- A record of at least three improvements, each naming the cause (unclear task, missing context, missing format or conflicting instructions), the change made, and the new version number.
- A short introduction (5 to 8 sentences) for a colleague, with three safety rules, in one shared document or spreadsheet.

## Submission Checklist
- My library has 15 prompts for real, recurring tasks in my own job role.
- Each prompt is a template with placeholders only for the parts that change.
- Each prompt card has a name, version, purpose, pattern, template, inputs, example input, example output, test notes, and the tool and date of the last test.
- I tested every template at least twice, including one harder case.
- I checked every test output for facts, sources, bias and confidentiality, and scored accuracy, format and tone from 1 to 5.
- I recorded at least three improvements with the cause, the change and the new version number.
- No prompt, example or test contains personal, client or confidential data.
- My library is in one shared document or spreadsheet with a short introduction and three safety rules.
