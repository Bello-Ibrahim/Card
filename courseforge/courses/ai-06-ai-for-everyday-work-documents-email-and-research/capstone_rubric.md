# Capstone Rubric: Redesign Three Weekly Tasks with AI

## Task
Redesign three of your weekly work tasks with an AI assistant, following safe-use rules, and measure the time saved including time spent checking the output. You map the tasks and record "before" times in L10 (capstone step 1), build and improve reusable prompts in L11 (capstone step 2), and do the tasks with AI, measure them and write your summary in L12 (capstone step 3). Never paste personal or confidential data into free AI tools, and follow your employer's AI policy.

## Deliverables
- Three task maps: each task broken into steps, every step labelled "AI can draft", "AI can check" or "human only" with a reason, a safety note, and a measured "before" time in minutes.
- Three reusable prompt templates, each with a first version, a note on what went wrong in testing, and an improved version. No template contains personal or confidential data.
- A results table: for each task, before time, after time (including prompt writing, checking and editing), time saved, a quality note and the findings from the 4-step review checklist.
- A one-page summary covering time saved, quality of the results, the safety rules and employer policy you followed, and a decision for each task: keep, change or stop using AI, with a reason.

## Submission Checklist
- I chose 3 weekly tasks that are repeated, text-based and low-risk.
- Each task is broken into steps, and every step is labelled "AI can draft", "AI can check" or "human only" with a reason.
- I recorded a real "before" time for each task.
- I have a tested reusable prompt for each task, with a first version and an improved version.
- My prompts and templates contain no personal or confidential data.
- I checked my employer's AI policy (or noted that none exists) and followed it.
- My "after" times include writing prompts, checking and editing.
- I reviewed every output with the 4-step checklist and recorded what I found and fixed.
- I reported every result honestly, including any zero or negative time savings.
- My one-page summary gives a keep, change or stop decision for each task, with a reason.
