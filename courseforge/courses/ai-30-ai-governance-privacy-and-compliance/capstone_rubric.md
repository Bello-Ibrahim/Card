# Capstone Rubric: AI Governance Policy and Risk Register

**This capstone is an educational exercise and is not legal advice.** The policy and register you produce are learning documents about a fictional organisation. They must not be used in a real organisation without review by qualified legal advisers in each relevant country.

## Task
Draft an AI governance policy and an AI risk register for Velmora Tradeways Ltd, the fictional logistics and trade-payments company introduced in L11. Velmora uses AI and processes personal data in the EU (the Netherlands) and in Nigeria, so your documents must map to the EU AI Act, the GDPR and the NDPA. You build the inventory and risk register in L12 (capstone step 1), draft the policy in L13 (capstone step 2), and add monitoring and incident response and submit in L14 (capstone step 3).

Do not state article numbers, section numbers, deadlines, penalty figures or thresholds unless you have checked them against the official text. Use placeholders such as "[GDPR breach deadline]" instead. Use only fictional details, and do not paste personal or confidential data into any AI tool.

## Deliverables
- **An AI inventory** covering all six Velmora systems, with owner, purpose, personal data used, likely EU AI Act role and tier, and GDPR and NDPA role.
- **A risk register** of at least 7 risks (at least 6 from step 1, plus at least 1 incident-related risk from step 3), covering at least 4 systems, using this structure:

| Risk | AI system | Likelihood | Impact | Owner | Control | Review date | Law(s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cause, event and effect in one sentence | Name from the inventory | 1–5 | 1–5 | A role that can act | What is in place now (mark "planned" if not) | Frequency or date, based on risk level | EU AI Act / GDPR / NDPA |

- **An AI governance policy** of 2–3 pages (plus section 10) following this outline: 1. Purpose and scope; 2. Definitions; 3. Principles; 4. Roles and responsibilities; 5. Approval process; 6. Acceptable and prohibited uses; 7. Personal data rules (lawful basis, minimisation, transparency, rights, transfers, breach handling); 8. Assessment triggers (DPIA, AI Act assessments, classification review); 9. Training; 10. Monitoring and incidents; 11. Review cycle and ownership.
- **A change log** showing which section Claude drafted first and what you kept, changed, removed or added.
- **The line** "This document is an educational exercise and is not legal advice." on both documents.
