# Capstone Rubric: Brand Campaign Visual Set

## Task
Produce a consistent 12-image visual set for a real or fictional brand campaign, with a one-page style guide that documents the prompts, settings, edits and tool terms you checked. You plan the set and generate 3 test images in L11 (capstone step 1), then produce, finish and review all 12 images in L12 (capstone step 2). Describe style through visual attributes only; do not name living artists. Do not include real people's likeness without consent, other brands' logos or copyrighted characters, and do not paste confidential client information into any AI tool.

## Deliverables
- A one-page campaign plan: completed style guide template (palette with hex codes, lighting, framing, medium, mood, recurring elements, things to avoid), a style block, and a 12-image shot list with subject, format and use for each image.
- 12 finished images that mix at least 2 formats (for example 1:1, 9:16 and 16:9), with real text added in a design tool where needed.
- A prompt log for all 12 images: tool and settings, aspect ratio, full prompt, reference used, edits made, checklist result and file name.
- A quality check record: the flaws found with the L09 checklist, their likely causes and the fixes applied, including at least 2 before-and-after examples.
- A one-page style guide covering the style block, key settings, a summary of edits, the tool terms you checked (with the date you read them), your bias review and your disclosure plan.

## Submission Checklist
- My plan includes a completed style guide, a style block and a 12-image shot list.
- My prompts describe style through visual attributes and do not name living artists.
- I submitted 12 finished images in at least 2 formats.
- My prompt log covers all 12 images, with prompts, settings, references and edits.
- I checked every image with the five-point flaw checklist and included at least 2 before-and-after examples.
- I added real text, owned logos and exact colours in a design tool, not in the generator.
- I read the commercial-use terms of every tool I used on the official website and wrote down the date.
- My images show no real people's likeness without consent, no other brands' logos and no copyrighted characters.
- I reviewed who is shown in my set for bias and wrote my disclosure plan.
- My style guide fits on one page and is written in my own words.
