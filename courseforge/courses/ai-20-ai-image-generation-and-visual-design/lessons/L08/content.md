# L08 Finishing in Canva: Layouts, Resizing and Text

Course: AI-20 · Module: M2 · Objectives: O4 · Video: 5 min (screen demo)

## Hook
Ask an image generator to write "SUMMER SALE 20% OFF" and you may get "SUMER SAEL 2O% OF". AI images are often great backgrounds, but they are not finished designs. The last step happens in a design tool.

## Explanation
Image generators are good at pictures but often weak at exact text, logos and precise brand colours. They can misspell words, invent letters or change a logo's shape. For this reason, professional workflows follow one simple rule:

**Generate the picture with AI. Add real text, logos and exact colours in a design tool.**

Canva is a free-first design tool that works well for this. Four finishing tasks matter most:

1. **Placing images in templates or layouts.** Start with a blank design of the right size, or a template, and place your AI image as the background or in a frame.
2. **Adding text in brand fonts and colours.** Type the headline yourself. Enter your exact brand colour as a hex code, such as #0B5C8A, in the colour picker. Design tools follow hex codes exactly, unlike most generators.
3. **Removing backgrounds.** Canva has a background remover for cutting out a product or subject. In Canva it may be a paid feature. Adobe Firefly and other tools also offer free options, so check what your plan includes. [VERSION]
4. **Resizing for different platforms.** One campaign often needs a square post, a tall story and a wide banner. Canva can copy a design into new sizes, but the automatic resize option may require a paid plan. On the free plan, you can create a new design in the new size and copy your elements into it. [VERSION]

Canva also offers its own AI image features. These change often, and some are free while others are not. [VERSION] In this course we use Canva mainly for finishing.

**Analogy:** An AI image is like a freshly baked cake. It may taste excellent, but it is not ready for the party until someone adds the writing, the candles and the right plate. Canva is the decorating table.

**Safety note:** Do not upload confidential client files or photos of people without consent. Only use logos you own or have permission to use.

## Worked Example
Arjun runs a yoga studio in Pune, India. He has a calm AI image of an empty studio with morning light and wants a social post for a new class. The presenter follows these steps on screen. Check every menu name in the live tool before recording. [VERSION]

**Part A: Social post**
1. Open Canva and sign in with a free account. [VERSION]
2. Click Create a design and choose a square social post size, or enter custom dimensions such as 1080 × 1080 pixels. [VERSION]
3. Upload the AI image using the uploads panel and drag it onto the page. Resize it to fill the page. [VERSION]
4. Add a text box. Type the headline: "Sunrise Flow – Mondays 7 am".
5. Choose a clear font. Open the colour picker and type the brand hex code, for example #1F6F5C. [VERSION]
6. Place the headline in the empty space of the image so it does not cover the main subject.
7. Upload the studio logo, if Arjun has one, and place it small in a corner.

**Part B: Background removal (optional)**
1. Select a product image, such as a yoga mat photo.
2. Open the edit image options and find the background remover. Point out whether it is marked as a paid feature. [VERSION]

**Part C: Resize for story and banner**
1. On a paid plan, use the resize option to create copies at 1080 × 1920 (story) and a wide banner size. [VERSION]
2. On the free plan, create a new design at the story size, then copy and paste the image, headline and logo into it. [VERSION]
3. In each new size, move the headline so it stays clear and readable. In the tall story, move it higher; in the wide banner, move it to the side.
4. Download each design as PNG. [VERSION]

## Common Mistake
The most common mistake is to accept an automatic resize without checking it. Text can end up over a face, a product can be cut in half, or the logo can fall off the edge. Always review each size by eye. Another mistake is to ask the generator for text and logos and then try to fix the spelling. Leave that space empty in the image and add text in Canva instead.

## Key Takeaways
1. Generate the picture with AI, then add real text, logos and exact brand colours in a design tool such as Canva.
2. Background removal and automatic resizing are useful, but which features are free changes often, so check your plan.
3. Review every resized version by eye, because text and subjects can move to the wrong place.

## Hands-on Exercise
**Task:** Place one of your images in a Canva social post with a headline in your brand colours, then resize it for a story format and a banner.
**Tools:** Canva (free plan); one image from your earlier exercises. [VERSION]
**Steps:**
1. Choose your brand colours and write down their hex codes. If you do not have a brand, invent 2 colours.
2. Create a square social post in Canva and place your image on it.
3. Add a headline of no more than 8 words in one of your brand colours.
4. Add a small logo or brand name in a corner, using only a logo you own or a simple text version.
5. Create a story version (1080 × 1920 pixels) and a banner version. Use the resize option if your plan includes it, or create new designs and copy the elements. [VERSION]
6. Adjust the layout in each size so the headline is clear and the subject is not covered.
7. Download all 3 versions.
**What good looks like:** Three designs from one image (square, story and banner), each with a correctly spelled headline in exact brand colours, a clear subject that is not covered by text, and no elements cut off at the edges.
**Time:** about 25 minutes
