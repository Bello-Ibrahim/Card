# Capstone Rubric: AI-Assisted Client Workflow from Proposal to Invoice

## Task
Build and test an AI-assisted client workflow for your own service, from proposal to invoice, with templates, prompts, at least one automation and a personal AI-use policy. You map the workflow in L09 (capstone step 1), build and test an automation in L10 (step 2), judge each AI and automation step in L11 (step 3), and document the full workflow and run one test client through it in L12 (step 4). If you have no clients yet, use a realistic example client. Do not paste confidential client information or personal data into AI tools, and use only example data when testing automations. AI drafts of contracts, invoices and tax material are not legal or tax advice; check those points with an official source or a qualified professional in your country.

## Deliverables
- A workflow map (spreadsheet or document) with 10 to 15 steps from proposal to invoice, each labelled "AI draft", "Automation" or "Human only", with a reason, and the four checklist answers and a keep, change or remove decision for each AI or automation step.
- A proposal template, an onboarding pack (welcome email, questionnaire, checklist), three delivery prompt templates, and an invoice template with a payment reminder.
- One working automation built with Zapier or n8n, with screenshots, a record of 3 tests with example data, and a note on how you will check it each week.
- Your one-page AI-use policy for client work.
- A test-run record of one client (real with permission, or example) from proposal to invoice, and a one-page evaluation of what worked, what failed and what you changed.

## Submission Checklist
- My workflow map covers every stage from proposal to invoice, and each step has a label and a reason.
- Every AI or automation step has the four checklist answers and a keep, change or remove decision.
- My proposal template, onboarding pack, three prompt templates and invoice template are in one folder with an overview page.
- My automation works, and I tested it 3 times with example data only.
- I attached screenshots of the automation and one test result.
- My AI-use policy says what I will and will not paste into AI tools, how I check work and what I tell clients.
- I did not paste confidential client information or personal data into any AI tool.
- I listed the contract, invoice and tax points I checked with an official source or professional in my country.
- I ran one test client through the full workflow and recorded times and problems.
- My evaluation shows at least one change I made based on the test.
