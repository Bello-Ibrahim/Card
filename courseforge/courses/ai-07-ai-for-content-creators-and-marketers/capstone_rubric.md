# Capstone Rubric: 30-Day Multi-Channel Content Campaign

## Task
Plan and produce a complete 30-day, multi-channel content campaign for a real or sample brand. You build the calendar in L12 (capstone step 1), produce the first 7 days of assets in L13 (capstone step 2), improve the plan with results data in L14, and review and present the campaign in L15 (capstone step 3). Use a real brand only with permission and only public information; otherwise use an unnamed, sample brand. Do not imitate a named living artist, writer or existing brand, and do not generate images of real people, logos or copyrighted characters. Do not paste personal or confidential data into AI tools.

## Deliverables
- A campaign summary: one measurable goal (with a number and a date), a short audience persona based on real or clearly labelled evidence, 3 content pillars and a posting rhythm.
- A 30-day content calendar covering at least 3 channels, with these columns: Day, Date, Week theme, Channel, Format, Pillar, Topic / hook, Call to action, Asset status, Owner, Notes.
- Finished sample assets for days 1 to 7, including at least one from each channel, at least one Canva visual and at least one short video with checked captions.
- At least one core piece repurposed into different formats, with one sentence per format explaining what changed for that audience.
- An asset log for every sample asset: tools used, prompt or source, AI-generated parts, licence checked, disclosure needed, review status.
- Improvement notes from L14: 3 AI suggestions based on the sample or real results table, each accepted, changed or rejected with a reason, and the calendar rows you updated.
- Review notes: the 5-point checklist results (accurate, on-brand, original, inclusive, legally safe), your disclosure rule, what you changed and any open questions.

## Submission Checklist
- My goal has a number and a date, and my persona marks what is confirmed and what still needs checking.
- My 30-day calendar covers at least 3 channels and has every template column filled in.
- I included finished assets for days 1 to 7, with at least one from each channel.
- Every caption and email has at least one sentence in my own words, and every video caption was checked word by word.
- I did not imitate a named artist, writer or brand, and my visuals show no real people, logos or copyrighted characters without permission.
- Every factual claim is linked to a source or removed.
- My asset log has a row for every asset, with licence and disclosure decisions.
- I applied my disclosure rule and checked current tool terms for commercial use, with the date I checked.
- I recorded 3 AI improvement suggestions with my decision and reason for each, and updated my calendar.
- I did not paste personal or confidential data into any AI tool.
