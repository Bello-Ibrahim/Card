## Production notes for this lesson

- Screen demo tools: Claude or ChatGPT for the script and shot list, then CapCut for editing. Record CapCut on a desktop or phone, using a demo account with no personal data visible.
- [VERSION] CapCut features (auto-captions, templates, caption styles, music library and its licence terms), menu names and plan limits must be checked against the live tool before recording. Content.md notes that menu names may differ.
- [REGION] CapCut availability differs by country. Confirm availability in the target markets (en, fr, pt, ar) before recording; if it is not available in a market, name a free alternative editor on the lesson page.
- [VERSION] Free-tier access to Claude and ChatGPT must be checked before recording.
- The five practice clips must be filmed by the team: a small plant being repotted on a shop counter, vertical, near a window, with no people's faces unless they have agreed to appear. Mateus and the plant shop in São Paulo are fictional; no real shop name or logo in shot.
- The auto-caption errors ('soul' for 'soil' and a misspelled plant name) should be created in the demo if CapCut does not produce them naturally, and shown clearly being corrected.
