## Production notes for this lesson

- Screen demo tools: Canva (free plan) for the brand kit, template and layout, and Google Gemini (image generation in the Gemini app) as the free image generator, per DECISIONS.md. Content.md step 4 uses Canva's own AI image feature; the demo generates the image in Gemini and uploads it to Canva instead. Canva's AI image feature may be shown briefly as an alternative if it is on the free plan.
- [VERSION] Canva AI feature names, brand kit availability on the free plan, menu names and plan limits must be checked against the live tool before recording. If the brand kit is not on the free plan, show the colour codes and fonts being set by hand instead.
- [VERSION] Gemini image generation limits and licence terms, and whether its images may be used commercially, must be checked before recording. Show the terms check being written in the asset log as a real step.
- Gemini adds a visible or invisible mark to generated images on some plans [VERIFY]; do not crop out any visible watermark in the demo.
- Fatou and the textile shop in Dakar are fictional. Use a demo Canva account with no personal data visible. No real people, logos, copyrighted characters or named artists' styles in any prompt or image.
- The customer review on the quote card must be an invented sample review, shown with the label 'sample'.
