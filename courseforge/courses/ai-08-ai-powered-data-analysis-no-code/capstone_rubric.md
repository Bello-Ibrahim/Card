# Capstone Rubric: Five-Slide Business Insight Report

## Task
Analyse a real business dataset with AI tools and present a five-slide insight report with charts, a clear recommendation and the limits of the analysis. You explore the data and keep a findings log in L12 (capstone step 1), plan the storyline in L13 (step 2), and build and check the slides in L14 (step 3). Use your own work data only if it is anonymised and you have permission; otherwise use a public dataset and note its source and licence. Do not upload personal or confidential data to AI tools.

## Deliverables
- A five-slide report (Google Slides or a similar free tool), shared view-only or exported as PDF, with slides for: the question, the key finding, the evidence charts, the recommendation, and the limits of the analysis.
- At least one speaker note that explains the limits of the analysis in more detail.
- The cleaned dataset (or a link to it) with a cleaning log and the source or permission note.
- A findings log with 3 measurable data questions and at least 5 confirmed findings, each showing how it was found and how it was checked, plus any rejected findings with reasons.
- A number check table: every number in the slides, its source, and "match" or "fix".

Total: 100

## Submission Checklist
- My dataset is a public dataset with a noted licence, or my own anonymised data that I have permission to use.
- I did not upload names, contact details, ID numbers or other personal or confidential data to any AI tool.
- I kept an untouched original and a cleaning log.
- I wrote 3 measurable data questions with the columns each one needs.
- My findings log has at least 5 confirmed findings, each with a specific check, and lists any rejected findings with reasons.
- My report has exactly five slides: question, key finding, evidence, recommendation, limits.
- Every chart title states a finding that my data supports.
- My recommendation is specific and says how to measure whether it worked.
- I added a speaker note that explains the limits of my analysis.
- I checked every number on every slide, including titles and notes, in a check table.
