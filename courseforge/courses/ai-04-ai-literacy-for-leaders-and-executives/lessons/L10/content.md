# L10 Your 90-Day AI Adoption Roadmap

Course: AI-04 · Module: M2 · Objectives: O4, O6 · Video: 5 min

## Hook
You now have use cases, a readiness score, a risk register, a policy draft, vendor questions and a pilot plan. On their own, they are six documents. Joined in the right order, they become a plan your team can follow from Monday.

## Explanation
A 90-day roadmap turns good ideas into action in three 30-day phases.

**Days 0–30: Prepare.** Get people, policy and data ready. Name a sponsor and a project owner. Share the acceptable-use policy. Train the pilot team. Check the data and take the baseline. Complete vendor due diligence and contract terms.

**Days 31–60: Pilot.** Run the pilot and measure it. Hold weekly check-ins, log problems and review risks against your register. Adjust small things quickly, but do not change the success measures.

**Days 61–90: Decide and scale.** Review results against the baseline. Make the go, adjust or stop decision. Communicate what you learned. Plan the next steps, such as scaling the pilot or preparing the next use case.

**Bringing staff with you.** People accept change more easily when they understand why it is happening and what it means for them. Involve staff in choosing and testing use cases. Be honest about how roles may change. Train people before you expect results. Give a clear way to raise concerns, and show that you act on them.

**Communicating the plan.** Share a short message at each phase: why you are doing this, what will happen, what it means for each group and how people can give feedback. Tell the board or senior team what you will decide and when. Explain risks openly; hiding them damages trust later.

Before scaling, confirm with your legal or compliance team that the wider use still fits the rules that apply in each country where you operate. [REGION]

**Analogy:** A 90-day roadmap is like a ship's voyage plan. You prepare the crew and supplies before leaving, check your position regularly during the trip and decide at the port whether to continue further. The plan can change, but everyone knows the route and who is steering.

## Worked Example
Tran Minh Anh is managing director of a hypothetical food manufacturer in Vietnam with 200 staff. Her priority use case is AI support for forecasting weekly ingredient orders.

- **Days 0–30:** the supply chain manager owns the project. The IT manager cleans two years of order data. The team completes vendor questions and agrees contract terms. Risk: poor data quality; control: a data check before the pilot. Success measure: baseline of ordering errors recorded.
- **Days 31–60:** buyers use the forecast as advice and still place every order themselves. Risk: buyers trust the forecast too much; control: weekly review of large differences. Success measures: ordering errors, waste and buyer satisfaction.
- **Days 61–90:** the leadership team reviews results and decides to adjust and extend the pilot to a second factory. Minh Anh presents the results to all staff and starts preparing the next use case.

**Capstone rubric.** Your roadmap is assessed on five criteria: use-case prioritisation, risks and controls, pilot and vendor plan, roadmap structure with owners and measures, and people and communication. The full rubric is on the course page.

## Common Mistake
Many roadmaps are lists of technology to buy. A strong roadmap is mostly about people, decisions and evidence: who owns each step, what will be measured, which risks are controlled, and how staff will be informed. If your roadmap has no owners or decision dates, it is a wish list, not a plan.

## Key Takeaways
1. A 90-day roadmap has three phases: prepare, pilot, and decide and scale.
2. Every phase needs goals, owners, risks and controls, success measures and communication.
3. Bringing staff with you, through involvement, training and honest communication, is part of the plan, not an extra.

## Hands-on Exercise
**Task:** Capstone step 2: write your 90-day AI adoption roadmap, including goals, use cases, risks and controls, owners, measures and a communication plan.
**Tools:** The CertifAI roadmap template in this lesson, in a document or spreadsheet; your work from L04 to L09. Optional: Claude or ChatGPT to review your draft for gaps, with names and confidential details removed.
**Steps:**
1. Write one overall goal for the 90 days.
2. Add your one or two priority use cases and your scores from L04.
3. Fill in the template for each phase, using your pilot plan from L09.
4. Add the top risks and controls from your risk register, with owners.
5. Add success measures and the decision date.
6. Write a communication plan: audience, message, channel and timing for each phase.
7. Check your roadmap against the capstone rubric before you submit.
**What good looks like:** A clear roadmap of two to three pages, with every template row filled for each phase, named owner roles, specific measures, practical risk controls and a realistic communication plan.
**Time:** about 60 minutes
