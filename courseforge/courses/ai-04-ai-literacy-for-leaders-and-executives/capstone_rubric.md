# Capstone Rubric: 90-Day AI Adoption Roadmap

## Task
Write a 90-day AI adoption roadmap for your own team or organisation, based on prioritised use cases, a risk register and a pilot plan. You write the pilot plan in L09 (capstone step 1) and the full roadmap in L10 (capstone step 2), using the CertifAI roadmap template with three phases: days 0–30 (prepare), days 31–60 (pilot) and days 61–90 (decide and scale). Do not include personal data or confidential figures; describe people by role and use ranges or placeholders where needed.

## Deliverables
- A use-case scoring table with at least five use cases scored on value and feasibility, and your one or two priority use cases marked (from L04).
- A risk register with at least five risks, each rated for likelihood and impact, with one control and one owner (from L06).
- A one-page pilot plan with scope, baseline, three success measures with targets, duration, decision date, feedback plan and the key vendor questions you need answered in writing (from L08 and L09).
- A 90-day roadmap of two to three pages using the CertifAI template, with goals, key actions, owners, risks and controls, success measures and communication for each phase.

## Criteria
| Criterion | Objective | Excellent | Good | Developing | Not yet | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Use-case prioritisation | O3 | At least five use cases scored on value and all four feasibility factors, with short reasons; priority choice is clearly justified and reflects readiness gaps. | Five use cases scored with a reasonable priority choice and some reasons. | Fewer than five use cases, or scores without reasons. | No scoring or no clear priority. | 20 |
| Risks and controls | O4 | At least five specific risks covering several categories, realistic ratings, a practical control and an owner for each, and top risks built into the roadmap phases. | Five risks with ratings, controls and owners, mostly linked to the use case. | Risks are general, or controls or owners are missing for some. | No risk register. | 20 |
| Pilot plan and vendor due diligence | O5 | Pilot has clear scope, measurable baseline, three measures with targets covering value, quality or risk, and people, a fixed decision date, and specific vendor questions to get in writing. | Pilot plan has a baseline, measures and a decision date; some vendor questions included. | Measures are vague, the baseline is missing, or no vendor questions. | No pilot plan. | 20 |
| Roadmap structure, owners and measures | O6 | All three phases complete in the template, with realistic actions, owner roles, success measures and a go, adjust or stop decision point. | All three phases present with most rows filled and owners named. | Phases present but several rows empty, or no decision point. | No phased roadmap. | 25 |
| People and communication | O4, O6 | Clear plan to involve and train staff, an honest message for each audience and phase, and a way for staff to raise concerns. | Communication plan covers main audiences and phases. | Communication is mentioned but not planned by audience or phase. | No communication or staff plan. | 15 |
