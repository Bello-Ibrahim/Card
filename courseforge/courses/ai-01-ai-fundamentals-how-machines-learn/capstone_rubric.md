# Capstone Rubric: Train and Explain an Image Classifier

## Task
Train a simple image classifier with Google Teachable Machine and write a one-page explanation of how it learned. You build the classifier in L11 (capstone step 1) and write the explanation in L12 (capstone step 2). Use objects, such as types of fruit or pens vs. cups. Do not use photos of other people without their clear consent.

## Deliverables
- A trained Teachable Machine image model with at least 2 named classes and at least 20 varied images per class.
- A test table with 10 new images that were not used in training, showing the true class, the predicted class, the confidence and "correct" or "wrong" for each.
- Two screenshots: one of your classes and one of a test result.
- A one-page written explanation covering: how the model learned, how you tested it, where it failed and why, your answers to the 5-question trust checklist, and one suggested improvement.

## Criteria

Total: 100

## Submission Checklist
- My model has at least 2 named classes and at least 20 varied images per class.
- I used objects, not photos of other people without their consent.
- I tested with 10 new images that were not used in training, and recorded every result, including mistakes.
- I attached a screenshot of my classes and a screenshot of a test result.
- My page uses the terms data, features, labels, training and prediction.
- I explained at least 2 failures and a likely reason for each.
- I answered all 5 trust checklist questions for my own model.
- I suggested one specific improvement to the data.
- My explanation fits on one page and is written in my own words.
